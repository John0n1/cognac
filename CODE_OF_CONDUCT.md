# Code of Conduct

Cognac welcomes technical disagreement, direct feedback, and rigorous review. Participation must still remain professional and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas, code, evidence, and reproducible behavior rather than attacking people
- give technical criticism directly and constructively
- assume neither expertise nor inexperience from identity or background
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information
- respect project security boundaries and responsible-disclosure requests
- accept maintainer decisions about scope even when disagreeing with them
- keep compatibility reports factual and reproducible

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation
- discriminatory or demeaning personal attacks
- sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention
- publishing private or identifying information without permission
- deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated off-topic antagonism
- encouraging contributors to conceal security-impacting behavior in reviews
- retaliation against someone for reporting a security or conduct concern

Strong criticism of code, architecture, project decisions, or technical claims is not harassment by itself. Keep that criticism about the work.

## Project scope and security boundaries

Cognac is a compatibility and orchestration project. Discussions about reverse engineering, compatibility, virtualization, operating systems, anti-cheat architecture, DRM, and security mechanisms are legitimate when they are relevant to understanding or improving compatibility.

Project spaces are not a venue for distributing malware, stolen credentials, cheats, piracy tooling, access-control bypasses, or instructions whose purpose is defeating security boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions or discussions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

When practical, moderation will be proportional to the behavior and will distinguish a technical disagreement from personal misconduct.

## Reporting conduct concerns

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull-request discussion.

For harassment, threats, privacy violations, or another concern that should not be public, contact the maintainer privately through the contact methods on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public conduct report.

## Scope

This policy applies to Cognac-controlled project spaces and to public conduct by a participant when they are explicitly representing Cognac.
